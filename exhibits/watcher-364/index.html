TEXT STATES · 1 ENOCH 74 · 4Q319

Six Jubilees of 364-day years are 293 solar years — to within an hour

294 × 364 = 107,016 days. 293 tropical years of 365.2422 days = 107,015.96 days. The difference is 0.04 of a day. At the close of the six-Jubilee cycle that 4Q319 runs, every date of the written year has come back to the season it started in, having walked once round the whole sun. 4Q319 states the 294-year cycle; 1 Enoch 72 and 74 state 364 and 1,092; Jubilees 6 states the year is 364 days and that the moon runs ten days early. No inspected text states the seasonal return. That identity is arithmetic, and it is ours.

ARITHMETIC · WITHIN THE CYCLE

Inside the cycle the dates travel

One Jubilee of 49 written years is 17,836 days; 49 solar years are 17,896.9. Each Jubilee the calendar falls 61 days behind the sun, and the six Jubilees together fall exactly one year behind, which is why they close. Three written years are 1,092 days: 37 lunar months less 0.63 of a day, which is the 1,062 + 30 of 1 Enoch 74. 4Q320–321 keep the moon’s phases in the tables for that reason.

COMPUTED · NISAN 14 IN 30 CE

Friday 7 April 30 · JDN 1,732,112

Conjunction Wednesday 22 March 30 at about 20:10. At sunset on the 23rd the moon was some 22 hours old and not yet visible; the first crescent was seen on the evening of Friday 24 March, so Nisan 1 began at that sunset and Nisan 14 was Friday 7 April. Full moon followed on Thursday 6 April at about 22:00. Humphreys and Waddington (1983) reach the same date with the same visibility call.

COMPUTED · NISAN 14 IN 33 CE

Friday 3 April 33 · JDN 1,733,204

Conjunction Thursday 19 March 33 at about 13:00. The crescent was visible on the evening of Friday 20 March (about 29 hours old), so Nisan 1 began at that sunset and Nisan 14 was Friday 3 April. Full moon that same evening at about 17:15, and it rose over Jerusalem in partial eclipse (Humphreys and Waddington 1983; Schaefer 1990 argues the eclipsed portion was too low to be seen). The two Fridays are 1,092 days apart.

WHAT THE 1,092 WEIGHS

Both dates are Fridays because the Gospels record the day of the week; that is testimony, not arithmetic. Three Julian years are 1,095 or 1,096 days, so any two Fridays three years apart in the same season are either 1,092 or 1,099 days apart. These two are 1,092, which is 3 × 364 and 156 × 7 exactly. The Enochic count adds one fact to the testimony: on a continuously counted 364-day year the two candidate dates fall on the same day of the same month. It does not decide between them, and it is not claimed to.

TEXT STATES · 4Q320 · 4Q326 · JUBILEES 6

The written year’s own Passover is a Tuesday

The year opens on the fourth day, the day the lights were made (Genesis 1:14–19; 4Q320 and 4Q326 open year one on Wednesday). Count forward: 1/14 is always a Tuesday, 1/15 always a Wednesday. In this calendar the Passover lamb is eaten on Tuesday night, every year, for ever.

TEXT STATES · THE GOSPELS

Two Passovers in one week

Matthew, Mark and Luke record the Passover meal eaten the evening before the crucifixion. John records the crucifixion on the preparation day of the Passover (John 19:14), the lamb not yet eaten. Annie Jaubert (La date de la Cène, 1957) read the two together through the 364-day year: the meal on the calendar’s Tuesday, the arrest that night, and the Friday death under the Temple’s lunar reckoning — three days that hold the two trials, the hearing before Herod, and Pilate’s judgment that a single night cannot. That is her reading; the texts state the two Passovers.

THE 294 / 300 COMPARISON

Jubilees defines a Jubilee as 49 years, making six Jubilees 294. Genesis 5:22 states that Enoch walked with God for 300 years; Genesis does not call those years Jubilees. Dividing that separate 300-year span by the six units named in Jubilees produces 50. This is a cross-text calculation; 4Q319 closes its own cycle at 294.

DIRECT WITNESS

Six thousand

Sanhedrin 97a, Barnabas 15, Irenaeus, Hippolytus, and Lactantius map creation’s six workdays onto six millennia followed by Sabbath time. 2 Enoch instead gives a 7,000-year revolution and an unmeasured eighth age.

DISTINCT TEXTUAL FAMILIES

Seventy / seventy-two

1 Enoch counts seventy shepherds and checks the sum itself: 12, 35, 58, 70. Greek nation lists, Aristeas, Luke’s manuscript split, and 3 Enoch carry their own 70/72 lines; none states a seventy-two-year Enochic unit.

EXPLICIT + DERIVED WITNESSES

Four hundred thirty-two thousand

Berossus’s transmitted king list gives 432,000 years to the Flood. The Viṣṇu Purāṇa derives 432,000 for Kali. Grímnismál supplies factors that equal 432,000 only under the decimal-hundred reading; a long-hundred reading gives 614,400.

THE SOURCE BOUNDARY

Twenty-five thousand nine hundred twenty

360 × 72 = 25,920 exactly, but the inspected ancient Enochic, Qumran, and Ptolemaic witnesses do not state it. Ptolemy’s rate yields 36,000; Riccioli states the exact 25,920 in 1651.

THE UNATTESTED BUT EXACT PRODUCT

6,000 × 72 = 432,000. The multiplication is real. The inspected ancient witnesses keep 6,000 and 72 in separate textual families; no source among them states this equation. The Indic corpus independently carries 432,000 → 4,320,000 → 25,920,000 inside its own system.
