Exhibit The chart the atlas cites

Adams's Synchronological Chart, 1881

The World atlas places the book's story on Sebastian C. Adams's chronology: Creation at 4004 BC, the Flood at 2348, the dispersion at 2247, Abraham's call at 1921. Those are Adams's printed placements on Ussher's reckoning, not dates written in Genesis. Here is the chart itself, sheet by sheet, so the placements can be read where he printed them.

The images are the David Rumsey Map Collection's scan of the 1881 edition, served from its own image service and cut only where the collection cut them: its ten page sheets plus the title and text pages. Nothing has been redrawn. Where a year is quoted below, it was read off the sheet and is given as printed.
