THE 294 / 300 COMPARISON

Jubilees defines a Jubilee as 49 years, making six Jubilees 294. Genesis 5:22 states that Enoch walked with God for 300 years; Genesis does not call those years Jubilees. Dividing that separate 300-year span by the six units named in Jubilees produces 50. This is a cross-text calculation; 4Q319 closes its own cycle at 294.

Do not erase the fractures

The contradictions are evidence.

A transmission conflict exposes competing clocks, stages of a tradition, or attempts to make inherited numbers fit. The record stays open at the break.

1 ENOCH 74

Thirty or fifty?

Verses 10–11 say the moon loses 30 days in five years. Verses 13–16 total 1,820 solar against 1,770 lunar days: a 50-day loss. Both readings remain on the table.

1 ENOCH 90

Seventy or seventy-two?

The Ethiopic at 90:1 reads 37 shepherds; editions often emend it to 35 so 35 + 23 + 12 equals the repeatedly announced 70. The unaltered numeral yields 72.

4Q208–209

364, 360, or 354?

The Aramaic fragments preserve gates, lunar fractions, and 29/30-day months. Competing reconstructions build a 364-day triennial system or a 354-day moon against an ideal 360-day sun.

4Q320–321

What are X and dwq?

The notations have been read as full moon, first crescent, lunar invisibility, last visibility, or the day after full moon. The fragment data outlive every proposed key.

DAY BOUNDARY

Sunrise and sunset

CD 10, 4Q503, Jubilees, MMT, Temple Scroll, and the mishmarot dates preserve sunset reckoning; other sequences put morning first. One reconstruction of 4Q320 runs both counts together.

2 ENOCH 13–16

365¼ and 365½

The Slavonic witness gives 365¼ for the solar gates, later 365½, a 354-day moon, a stated twelve-day deficit, a 19-year lunar cycle, and a 532-year great cycle. Its figures do not collapse into one modern table.

DIRECT WITNESS

Six thousand

Sanhedrin 97a, Barnabas 15, Irenaeus, Hippolytus, and Lactantius map creation’s six workdays onto six millennia followed by Sabbath time. 2 Enoch instead gives a 7,000-year revolution and an unmeasured eighth age.

DISTINCT TEXTUAL FAMILIES

Seventy / seventy-two

1 Enoch announces seventy shepherds even though one preserved subtotal reads 37 and produces 72. Greek nation lists, Aristeas, Luke’s manuscript split, and 3 Enoch carry their own 70/72 lines; none states a seventy-two-year Enochic unit.

EXPLICIT + DERIVED WITNESSES

Four hundred thirty-two thousand

Berossus’s transmitted king list gives 432,000 years to the Flood. The Viṣṇu Purāṇa derives 432,000 for Kali. Grímnismál supplies factors that equal 432,000 only under the decimal-hundred reading; a long-hundred reading gives 614,400.

THE SOURCE BOUNDARY

Twenty-five thousand nine hundred twenty

360 × 72 = 25,920 exactly, but the inspected ancient Enochic, Qumran, and Ptolemaic witnesses do not state it. Ptolemy’s rate yields 36,000; Riccioli states the exact 25,920 in 1651.

THE UNATTESTED BUT EXACT PRODUCT

6,000 × 72 = 432,000. The multiplication is real. The inspected ancient witnesses keep 6,000 and 72 in separate textual families; no source among them states this equation. The Indic corpus independently carries 432,000 → 4,320,000 → 25,920,000 inside its own system.
