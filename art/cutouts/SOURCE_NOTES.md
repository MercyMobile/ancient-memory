# Illustration source notes

These transparent paper-theatre images are artistic reconstructions. The references below anchor the features that ancient texts or excavated architecture specify; faces, fabrics, colors, and fine construction details are imagined.

- **Written witnesses:** The scroll and clay tablet represent the manuscript and cuneiform records presented in the Witnesses chapter (for example Qumran and Nineveh). The drawn marks are illustrative, not a transcription of a particular artifact. Generated with the built-in image tool from a prompt for a transparent, layered parchment scroll and cuneiform tablet cutout.
- **Watcher:** Genesis 6:1–4 and 1 Enoch 6 place the descent and oath at Mount Hermon. Neither gives a portrait. The veiled humanlike form and luminous paper layers are visual interpretation; wings, armor, and masks were deliberately excluded.
- **Gilgamesh:** The *Epic of Gilgamesh* describes a magnificent, exceptionally powerful, partly divine king; the Qumran *Book of Giants* also names Gilgamesh. The face and clothing are imagined, and the image does not add six fingers. [University of Chicago translation](https://isac.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/misc_gilgamesh.pdf).
- **Goliath and Gath:** 1 Samuel 17 describes Goliath's bronze helmet, scale armor, greaves, and spear. The six-fingered, six-toed man of Gath is a different, unnamed figure in 2 Samuel 21:20. [Text of 2 Samuel 21:20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2%20Samuel%2021%3A20&version=ESV).
- **Noah's ark:** Genesis 6:14–16 supplies 300 by 50 by 30 cubits, rooms, pitch, a side door, roof/opening, and three decks. The squared vessel follows those proportions; exterior joinery and window details are imagined. The [Ark Encounter](https://arkencounter.com/about/) is a modern full-scale interpretation based on a 20.4-inch cubit, measuring about 510 by 85 by 51 feet.
- **Tower:** Genesis 11:3–4 specifies brick, bitumen, a city, and a tower. The stepped mudbrick form draws on excavated Iraqi ziggurats and reconstructions of Etemenanki; no excavation establishes that a surviving ziggurat is the tower of Genesis 11. [French Ministry of Culture on Etemenanki architecture](https://archeologie.culture.gouv.fr/orient-cuneiforme/en/architecture-ziggurat); [British Museum excavation at Tello](https://www.britishmuseum.org/our-work/international/iraq-scheme/tello).
- **Serpents:** The garden uses an ordinary serpent in Genesis 3. The sea-serpent image is separate and draws on Leviathan passages such as Job 41 and Psalm 74:14.

The ark page also cites [Hong and colleagues’ original wave-tank study](https://creation.com/images/pdfs/tj/j8_1/j8_1_26-36.pdf). It tested relative safety of modeled hulls under assumptions; it did not prove a vessel unsinkable.

Independent checks: [University of Leicester physics students](https://journals.le.ac.uk/index.php/pst/article/view/2169) calculated buoyancy for a Genesis-proportioned box hull; [Harvard Natural Sciences Lecture Demonstrations](https://sciencedemonstrations.fas.harvard.edu/presentations/stability-flotation) tests the restoring behavior of a loaded rectangular model. A [Shearer & Associates naval-architecture review letter](https://asknoah.org/essay/the-past-of-our-future-secrets-of-noahs-ark) reports a separate digital stability analysis of a larger-cubit Ark reconstruction; it was commissioned for a religious manuscript and does not document a 90-degree roll. These are distinct tests of different questions.

The [Sivas Cumhuriyet University 2026 Durupınar project](https://www.cumhuriyet.edu.tr/haber/18597-universitemiz-onculugunde-yurutulen-nuhun-gemisi-arastirmalarinda-heyecan-verici-ilk-sonuclar) reported preliminary subsurface radar anomalies and a soil-carbon difference. The ark page says these findings warranted continued investigation and keeps this site research separate from engineering studies of reconstructed vessels.
